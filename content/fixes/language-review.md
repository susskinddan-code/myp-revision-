# Language content accuracy review (MYP 5)

Scope: french/spanish/mandarin ab initio, french/spanish/english Language and Literature sub-topic content (56 files, 168 sub-topics, 3,024 MCQs, 1,512 flashcards, 168 notes) and the 38 language practice papers in content/papers-myp5 (278 paper questions). content/gap has no language items.

Checks done: every French/Spanish/Mandarin example for accents, agreement, conjugation, pinyin tones and simplified characters; the stored MCQ answer and whether any other option is defensible; explanations; notes against MYP Language Acquisition phases and Language and Literature criteria A-D (Levels 1-8, eAssessment 1-7 shown separately); no DP/A-level/IGCSE scope; flashcards not swapped; paper mark-scheme totals equal question marks (no mismatches). The 10 fixes already in content/review/languages-fixes.md are in the working tree and are not repeated here; language-fixes.sql does not depend on them.

Result: content quality is high. No wrong MCQ answers were found. The errors below are one wrong claim in a note/flashcard, defensible distractors, ambiguous or misleading wording, and explanations that refer to option positions (options are re-ordered at load time, so these can point at the wrong option in the live DB).

Fixes are applied in place in the JSON and mirrored in content/fixes/language-fixes.sql (tested against a local Postgres loaded with the pre-fix content: all 42 statements UPDATE 1, re-run safe).

Dan may prefer to relabel them as "Step 1/2/3" for consistency.
