# eAssessment topic lists (from Dan's teacher notes, 2026-10-11) - use as coverage checklist

## Biology
Cells: tissues, organs, systems, structure and function, factors affecting human health, physiology, vaccination.
Organisms: habitat, ecosystems, interdependency, unity and diversity, energy transfer and nutrient cycles, carbon and nitrogen, classification.
Processes: photosynthesis, cell respiration (aerobic and anaerobic, word and chemical equations).
Metabolism: nutrition, digestion, biochemistry and enzymes, movement and transport (diffusion, osmosis, gas exchange, circulation, transpiration and translocation, homeostasis).
Evolution: life cycles, natural selection, mitosis and meiosis, reproduction, biodiversity, inheritance and variation, DNA.
Interactions with environment: tropism, senses, nervous system, receptors and hormones. With organisms: pathogens/parasites, predator/prey, food chains/webs, competition, speciation, extinction. Human interactions: influences, habitat change, pollution/conservation, overexploitation, mitigation.
Biotechnology: genetic modification, cloning (ethics), genome mapping, 3D tissue and organ printing.

## Chemistry
Matter: states, particle theory, diffusion, atomic structure and isotopes, electron configuration and valency.
Bonding: structure and bonding, formulas, conservation of mass, balancing, the mole and calculations, reaction kinetics and collision theory, equilibria/reversible reactions, energy changes, endo/exothermic, combustion of fuels.
Periodic table: metals/non-metals, transition metals, noble gases, trends, periods and groups.
IUPAC: naming and classifying alkanes, alkenes, alcohols, carboxylic acids, esters; structural formulas; food chemistry.
Pure and impure: mixtures (solutions, oils, alloys, emulsions); separation (filtration, distillation incl. crude oil, chromatography).
Atmosphere: gases, composition, testing and treatment, extraction/emission/environmental implications.
Types of reactions: acids and bases, neutral solutions, acid/base reactions, pH and indicators, salts (formation, uses), redox, reactivity series, extraction of metals, corrosion, electrochemical cells.

## History
Superpowers, empires and supra-national institutions; peace and conflict; significant individuals; independence and national identity; rights and social protest; industrialization, industry and labour; globalization (trade, aid, exchange, flows); intellectual and ideological movements; pioneers, innovators and developers; health and medicine; individual, household and daily life; social, cultural and artistic developments.

## Geography
Changing population (natural increase, structure, migration); sustainable management of urban systems; globalization; resource management (extraction, production, consumption, security); tourism; river and coastal environments; global climate change (causes, consequences, responses); natural disasters; tectonically active areas; sustainable management of natural environments (aquatic, deserts, forests, grasslands, Arctic/tundra); GIS.
